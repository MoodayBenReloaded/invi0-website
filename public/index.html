Frequently Asked Questions.

Lumus is an AI-powered account intelligence layer built by INVI0. It assigns one AI intelligence to each of your sales accounts - monitoring CRM data, emails, external signals, and open commitments in real time. It then drafts the next best action for your reps automatically. Think of it as giving every account its own dedicated analyst that never sleeps, never forgets, and always keeps your rep one step ahead.
Under 15 minutes. Lumus connects to your existing CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive), email, and calendar through standard integrations. There's no data migration, no complex configuration, and no new workflow to learn. Your reps connect their accounts and Lumus starts working in the background immediately - delivering the first account insights within hours.
Think of Lumus as an AI Account Executive assigned to each of your accounts. Every morning, your reps open their laptop and find: recent news about their accounts (funding rounds, leadership changes, product launches), open commitments from past meetings, risk signals on deals going cold, and ready-to-send follow-up emails. Instead of spending 1-2 hours researching before every call, your reps walk in fully prepared in seconds.
Your CRM stores data. Lumus makes it useful. Most CRM data sits untouched - reps log activities but rarely go back to analyze patterns across accounts. Lumus pulls your CRM data, combines it with external signals your reps would never find on their own, and turns all of it into prioritized actions. It connects to Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive in under 15 minutes. No migration, no new workflow to learn.
Gong records and analyzes calls. 6sense tracks buyer intent signals. Dealfront finds leads. Each solves one piece. Lumus is the layer that sits on top of all of it - it fuses your CRM data, external news, meeting commitments, and communication history into one intelligence file per account and then drafts the actual next steps. Your rep doesn't need to switch between five tabs and piece it together themselves. Lumus does that work.
Yes. All data is processed and stored on EU servers. We built Lumus DSGVO-compliant from day one because our first customers are German Mittelstand companies who require it. Raw data like call transcripts is analyzed and then deleted - we only keep the extracted insights. We provide an Auftragsverarbeitungsvertrag (AVV) and can walk your IT team through our security setup before you start.
