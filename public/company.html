Built by People Who Lived the Problem.

We spent years in B2B sales and saw the same thing everywhere: great reps wasting hours on manual research that AI should handle. So we built Lumus.

INVI0 stands for Invisible Intelligence. The best Account Executives at companies like Google don't win by making more calls - they win because they never miss a commitment, never get blindsided by news that matters, and walk into every meeting with deep research done. Lumus makes that the standard for every rep: one AI intelligence per account that tracks every detail, catches what humans forget, and brings the best out of each AE - so they can do what only they can do: build relationships and close deals. Based in Dusseldorf, building for European B2B.
